# 1100 6th St, Norco · church design draft

Interactive Three.js massing and floor-plan model for the proposed church at 1100 6th St, Norco, CA (C-4 Commercial zone).

- Live page: https://mrhaneul.github.io/sixth-st-3d/
- Floor plans follow slide 14 of the budget comparison deck (1F 100′ × 80′, 2F 50′ × 80′).
- Views: exterior, cutaway, six section cuts through both floors, 1st and 2nd floor plans.
- Panel: building position, roof heights, parking inputs, live C-4 zoning checks, room schedule, deck cross-check.

Lot outline, neighbors and street widths are traced from the listing aerial, not surveyed.
